Privacy Policy

At Snazzy Paws Grooming, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we keep it safe.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following information when you use our services:

  • Your name and contact details (email, phone number)
  • Your dog's name, breed, and grooming requirements
  • Booking details such as preferred date and time
  • Any additional notes you provide

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to:

  • Manage grooming appointments
  • Contact you about your booking
  • Improve our services

How We Protect Your Information

We take reasonable steps to keep your information secure. Your details are only accessed by authorised staff and are not shared with third parties unless required by law.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request a copy of the information we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct any inaccurate information
  • Request that we delete your information where appropriate

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or how your information is handled, please contact us at info@snazzypaws.co.nz.
