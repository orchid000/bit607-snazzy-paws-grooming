Privacy Statement

Snazzy Paws Grooming is committed to protecting your privacy. This statement explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, how we store it, and how you can access or correct your information.

Information We Collect

We collect personal information such as your name, contact details, and information about your dog when you make a booking or contact us through our website.

Why We Collect Your Information

We collect this information so we can schedule appointments, contact you about your booking, and provide grooming services tailored to your dog’s needs.

How Your Information Is Stored

Your information is stored securely and is only accessed by authorised staff. We keep your information only for as long as necessary to provide our services.

If You Choose Not to Provide Information

If you choose not to provide required information, we may not be able to complete your booking or provide certain services.

Accessing or Correcting Your Information

You have the right to request access to the personal information we hold about you, and to request corrections if you believe it is inaccurate.
